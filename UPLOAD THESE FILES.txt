This update adds Angi lead-fee tracking.

- Selecting Angi in New Work File reveals an optional Lead Fee amount.
- Existing Angi work files show Lead Tracking in Job Details.
- Use Edit Job Details to update the fee for an older Angi file.
- The fee is tracked separately and does not change existing project revenue or expense totals.
